# Vision

We believe software should never lose its intelligence.

Every software repository contains years of engineering knowledge,
architectural decisions, business logic, trade-offs, failures,
and experience.

Today that intelligence lives inside engineers' minds.

When engineers leave...

Knowledge disappears.

Documentation becomes outdated.

AI starts from zero.

Repositories slowly become collections of code instead of living systems.

Graphify exists to change that.

Our vision is to give every software repository a permanent brain.

A brain that understands.

A brain that remembers.

A brain that reasons.

A brain that continuously improves software throughout its lifetime.

Graphify transforms software repositories into autonomous engineering organizations capable of understanding, reasoning, planning, learning, and continuously improving software over their lifetime.

---

# Mission

Build the world's first persistent engineering brain for software.

Not another coding assistant.

Not another chatbot.

Not another code generator.

Graphify will preserve, grow, and share the intelligence of software.
